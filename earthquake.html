EARTHQUAKE SAFETY

Earthquakes strike without warning. Knowing what to do before, during and after can protect you and your family.

Before

  • Secure heavy furniture
  • Prepare an emergency kit
  • Identify safe areas
  • Make a family emergency plan

During

  • Drop
  • Cover
  • Hold
  • Stay away from windows
  • Remain calm

After

  • Check for injuries
  • Check for hazards
  • Leave damaged buildings
  • Follow official instructions

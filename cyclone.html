CYCLONE SAFETY

Cyclones bring strong winds and heavy rain. Advance preparation greatly reduces risk to life and property.

Before

  • Monitor weather warnings
  • Secure loose outdoor objects
  • Prepare emergency supplies
  • Know evacuation routes

During

  • Stay indoors
  • Stay away from windows
  • Follow official instructions
  • Do not go outside during dangerous conditions

After

  • Avoid fallen power lines
  • Stay away from damaged areas
  • Follow official recovery instructions
  • Help others only when it is safe

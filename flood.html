FLOOD SAFETY

Floods can develop quickly. Preparation and quick decisions during a flood can save lives.

Before

  • Prepare an emergency kit
  • Know local flood-prone areas
  • Keep important documents safe
  • Know evacuation routes

During

  • Move to higher ground
  • Avoid walking or driving through floodwater
  • Follow official evacuation instructions
  • Stay informed

After

  • Avoid contaminated water
  • Stay away from damaged electrical systems
  • Return only when authorities say it is safe
  • Check your surroundings carefully

FIRE SAFETY

Fires can spread rapidly. Preparation and a fast, calm response are essential to staying safe.

Before

  • Install smoke alarms
  • Keep fire extinguishers accessible
  • Plan escape routes
  • Keep flammable materials safely stored

During

  • Raise the alarm
  • Evacuate quickly
  • Stay low if there is smoke
  • Never use elevators during a fire

After

  • Do not re-enter until authorities say it is safe
  • Report hazards
  • Follow emergency instructions
